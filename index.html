What mPOS Does

⚠️ mPOS is not a consumer application. It is backend settlement infrastructure embedded directly into bank systems — similar to how Visa and Mastercard operate as invisible rails inside banking platforms. Customers never interact with mPOS directly.

Bank-Embedded Settlement RailmPOS sits inside bank infrastructure. The customer sees their bank. The bank uses mPOS.

Nigeria Side — Real Time Scan & Random Assignment

Screen 5 of 11 — Sender Agent Assigned

What is an Agent?

👤 Agents are accredited bank customers with available liquidity who have consented to participate in the mPOS network. They are not employees or external companies. They earn a share of the 1% fee per transaction.

What the Engine Checks — Sender Side

💡 For the sender agent, the engine queries and locks the agent's international ledger balance only — confirming they have sufficient ledger value to facilitate this transaction.

Real Time Scan — Nigeria

🔐 Security Protocol — Random Assignment Active
⚠️ Neither the sending customer nor the agent can select each other. The engine scans accredited Nigeria agents with a qualifying international ledger balance at the moment of the transaction — then randomly assigns one. This ensures ledger value stays in active circulation and prevents coordinated transaction routing.

The 0.2% Liquidity Correction Buffer

✅ Reserved locally in the Nigeria partner bank. Deployed automatically when corridor imbalance is detected. If the buffer alone is insufficient, the PPG OpenMarket layer activates liquidity facilitators on the receiving side to supply liquidity back into the stalling corridor.

LEDGER & CONVERSION

International Ledger — Balance & 3-Day Cycle

Screen 10 of 11 — Ledger Update & Agent Conversion

What is the International Ledger?

💡 The international ledger is an internal accounting record — not a separate currency, not a pool of money. Ledger balances are denominated in the agent's own local currency and exist solely for facilitating international transactions. The value never leaves the country — it is consumed as transactions are facilitated and must be replenished.
