What is an Agent?

👤 Agents are accredited bank customers with liquidity who have consented to participate in the mPOS network. They are randomly assigned by the engine — neither the sender nor the agent can select each other. This prevents coordinated attacks and money laundering.

Why No Cross-Border IB Exchange?

⚠️ UK agents cannot exchange IB directly with Nigeria agents. IB exchange only happens between agents within the same country. This prevents bad actors from buying IB across borders to cause shortages and corridor imbalance.

Step 9 of 11 — Ledger Update

What is the International Ledger?

💡 The international ledger is not a pool of money. It is a balance sheet of agent obligations. IB units mirror exactly the fiat paid or received. Agents hold IB temporarily and must convert it back to local fiat within 3 days — keeping value flowing and corridors balanced.
