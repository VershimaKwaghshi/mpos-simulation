⚠️ INFRASTRUCTURE NOTICE mPOS is not a consumer application. It is a backend settlement and execution infrastructure embedded directly into bank systems — similar to how Visa and Mastercard operate as invisible rails inside banking platforms. Nigeria Side — Real Time Random Assignment

Screen 5 of 11 — Sender Agent Assigned

What is an Agent?

👤 Agents are accredited bank customers with available liquidity who have consented to participate in the mPOS network. They are not employees or third-party companies. They are verified bank account holders whose liquidity facilitates transactions — and who earn a share of the 1% fee in return.

Real Time Agent Scan — Nigeria

🔐 Security Protocol — Random Assignment Active
⚠️ Neither the sending customer nor the agent can select each other. The engine scans all accredited Nigeria agents with qualifying NGN liquidity and a valid IB ledger age at the moment of the transaction — then randomly assigns one. This prevents coordinated attacks, money laundering rings, and agent manipulation.

The 0.2% Liquidity Correction Buffer

✅ Reserved locally in the Nigeria partner bank. Deployed automatically when corridor imbalance is detected — for example when Nigeria sends significantly more outbound than inbound over a sustained period. If the buffer alone is insufficient, PPG OpenMarket activates facilitators on the receiving side to supply liquidity back into the stalling corridor. This is a multi-layer imbalance correction system.

INTERNATIONAL LEDGER

Multi-Corridor Balance Sheet

Screen 10 of 11 — Ledger & IB Conversion

What is the International Ledger?

💡 The international ledger is not a pool of money. It is a real-time balance sheet of agent obligations across corridors. IB units mirror exactly the fiat paid or received. Agents hold IB temporarily and must convert it back to local fiat within 3 days — keeping value flowing and preventing corridor imbalance.

IB Conversion — 3 Day Rule

⚖️ Agent UK-007 holds IB 98.80 units. They must exchange this with another UK agent within 3 days to recover GBP fiat. Exchange is permitted only within the same country — never cross-border. This prevents bad actors from accumulating IB across corridors to cause shortages.
